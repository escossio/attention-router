# Synthetic fixtures

Alex is the example owner; Morgan and Taylor are fictitious contacts. Example email domains are reserved examples. Numeric WhatsApp identifiers are non-routable test data with zero-filled national components; they preserve parser shape, not real subscriber identity. Short number suffixes are artificial. No fixture is permission to send a message.

Loopback and service names in local examples are generic. Documentation IP ranges are examples, never production hosts. Internal operational histories and deployment overlays are intentionally excluded from this tree.
