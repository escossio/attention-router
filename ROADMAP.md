# Roadmap

This roadmap is directional and has no promised dates. It separates the
public prerelease baseline from ideas that are not implemented.

## Now

- **Implemented:** v0.1.0 public prerelease baseline, protected CI, security
  scanning, GHCR distribution, SBOM/provenance, and deterministic offline demo.
- **Implemented:** policy, explicit human-control boundary, outbox and delivery
  evidence concepts documented by the current architecture.

## Next

- **Planned:** stronger session context and lifecycle semantics.
- **Planned:** operational hardening and clearer provider abstractions.
- **Planned:** developer experience improvements for local setup, inspection and
  safe adapter development.

## Later

- **Conceptual:** management copilot capabilities.
- **Conceptual:** career intelligence and goal-engine concepts.
- **Conceptual:** organizational, enterprise authorization and governance
  concepts, subject to separate design and security review.

Planned and conceptual items are not claims that those capabilities exist in
the current release.
